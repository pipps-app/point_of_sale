## ## POSMENU: Front-End Operations

### ### POS (Point of Sale)
* **Financial Precision**: Implements integer-based "cents" math for all internal calculations to eliminate floating-point rounding errors.
* **Interactive Search**: Utilizes a live search interface with full keyboard support (Arrow keys for selection, Enter for adding to cart).
* **Calculation Workflow**: Applies item-specific adjustments first, then calculates global adjustments based on the resulting subtotal.
* **Documentation**: Leverages `Dompdf` for server-side PDF receipt generation and `PHPMailer` for automated digital distribution.
* **Inventory Control**: Automatically decrements the `quantity_on_hand` for every item sold upon transaction completion.

### ### POS Refund
* **Purpose**: Manages customer returns and the reversal of financial transactions.
* **Logic**: Reverses the financial flow established during initial sales to ensure balanced accounts and accurate historical reporting.

### ### POS Invoice
* **Detailed Processing**: Operates with the same high-precision engine as the POS module to process customer-specific invoices.
* **Documentation**: Regenerates full-detail invoices for distribution, including a comprehensive breakdown of itemized discounts and charges.

### ### POS Query
* **Purpose**: Serves as an administrative tool for searching and reviewing historical order data.
* **Visibility**: Provides a transparent view into past transactions for tracking, auditing, and reconciliation purposes.

### ### Item Price List
* **Purpose**: Displays a read-only list of current item names, codes, and their respective selling prices.
* **Distribution**: Includes an integrated "Email Item Price List" feature using a dedicated modal to send the catalog to specified recipients.

## ## CODEMENU: Administrative & Relationship Management

### ### Item
* **Master Catalog**: Centralizes the management of product codes, names, vendors, and categories.
* **Pricing Engine**: Features an automated calculation where $Selling Price = Cost + (Cost \times \frac{Markup}{100})$.
* **Data Integrity**: Uses specialized functions like `cleanPriceField` to remove commas and format decimal values for database storage.

### ### Client
* **Relationship Management**: Stores detailed customer profiles including names, addresses, emails, and notes.
* **Phone Formatting**: Automatically applies a standard (XXX) XXX-XXXX mask for home, cell, and work numbers to ensure data consistency.
* **Safety Constraints**: Prevents the deletion of any client record that is currently associated with booked slots in the system.

### ### Vendor
* **Supply Chain**: Manages the database of entities that supply inventory items.
* **Integration**: Vendors are linked directly to items in the master catalog for sourcing visibility.

### ### Item Category
* **Inventory Organization**: Groups products using unique category codes (e.g., for reporting or easier item navigation).
* **Logic Constraints**: Systems prevent the deletion of a category if it is currently linked to active items.

### ### Client Category
* **Customer Segmentation**: Organizes the customer base into logical groups.
* **Constraint**: Prevents the deletion of a category code if it is currently assigned to any client records.

### ### Discount
* **Price Reductions**: Manages active discounts with support for "fixed" or "percentage" types.
* **Flexibility**: Discounts can be configured globally or restricted specifically to individual items by ID.

### ### Charge
* **Additional Fees**: Manages transactional add-ons such as service fees or taxes.
* **Configuration**: Supports fixed or percentage-based rates that can apply to specific items or the total transaction subtotal.

### ### Company
* **Business Identity**: Configures the primary business details, including name, address, phone, and fax.
* **Receipt Customization**: Allows management to define three custom footer lines that appear on all system-generated documents.

## ## SECMENU: Security

### ### Change Password
* **Credentials Management**: Allows users to securely update their account passwords.
* **Access Control**: Integrated with `sessionvalidate.php` to ensure only the authenticated user can modify their specific security settings.
